⚠ This list is compiled automatically and may be incomplete.

Faculty names are gathered by a script that reads public UNC School of Medicine department pages, then matches each name against PubMed. That means:

  • People are missing. Anyone whose department page the script can't read, or who joined since the last weekly update, won't appear here.
  • "No indexed publications" often means we couldn't match the name — not that someone hasn't published. Common surnames and name changes are especially hard to match.
  • Publications may be attributed to the wrong person. Name matching is imperfect, particularly for common surnames.
  • Titles and departments may be out of date relative to the department's own page, which is always the authoritative source.

Before reaching out to a potential mentor, check their department profile and PubMed directly. If something here is wrong about you, or you'd like your entry removed, email jgbresearch@unc.edu — we'll fix or remove it, no explanation needed.
